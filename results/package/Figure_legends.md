# Figure legends

*Manuscript v2.0*

**Figure 1.** Selection of the external genome collection. Of 952 Staphylococcus capitis assembly records in the NCBI Assembly database, 614 were released from 2023 onward; paired GenBank and RefSeq records were collapsed to 307 genomes, 5 failed the independence or quality filters, and the S. capitis subsp. capitis type strain DSM 20326 (GCF_040739495.1) was removed on review as the outgroup of the discovery collection, giving a final set of 301 genomes.

**Figure 2.** cgMLST allelic distance to the Cluster L reference strain LNZR-1 in the two collections. Each point is one genome, jittered vertically; the horizontal axis is broken between 60 and 385 allelic differences, and all 921 genomes are plotted. In the discovery collection (n = 620) the lineage reaches 25 allelic differences and the nearest non-lineage genome sits at 398, a gap of 373. In the external collection (n = 301) seven-locus ST6 genomes extend to 29 — three of them beyond the 25-allele cutoff derived from the discovery set — and a single ST46 genome sits at 50, before a gap of 464 to the next genome at 514. Marker shape as well as colour distinguishes the groups, so the figure is readable in greyscale and under colour-vision deficiency.

**Figure 3.** Discovery against external performance for all 1,346 candidate targets. Each point is one candidate, placed by the number of non-lineage genomes it flagged in the discovery collection and the number it flagged in the external collection, with the 26 discovery-perfect candidates and ftsZ marked. The figure carries two things at once: how closely discovery performance predicts external performance (Spearman rho = 0.94), and the fact that no candidate combined complete external sensitivity with zero external false positives. Both axes use a symmetric log scale so that zero can be shown.
